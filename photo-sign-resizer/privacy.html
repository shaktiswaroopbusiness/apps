Privacy Policy – Photo & Sign Resizer

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Photo & Sign Resizer ("the app") resizes and compresses photos and signatures on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Photos you choose

Photos are picked with the Android system photo picker or taken with your camera only when you tap a button. All resizing happens on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to any server. Processed images are kept in the app's temporary cache until you save or share them. The app does not ask for permission to read your gallery.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The app has no account system and no server of its own.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and app interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Camera

Camera permission is requested only if you choose "Take a photo". The photo stays on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
