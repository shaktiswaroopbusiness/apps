Privacy Policy – Ghar Hisab

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Ghar Hisab ("the app") helps you keep a daily record of household services such as milk, maid, cook, driver, newspaper and water, and calculates monthly bills. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Your records stay on your phone

Everything you enter — service names, rates, salaries, daily marks, payments, notes, UPI IDs and phone numbers — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We have no server, no account system, and we never receive, upload, sell or share this data.

Backups

If you tap Backup, the app creates a file with your records and opens the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp). The app does not upload it anywhere by itself. Restore reads only the file you pick.

Sharing bills and UPI payments

When you tap Share bill, an image of that bill is passed to the app you choose. When you tap Pay by UPI, the app opens your UPI app (such as Google Pay, PhonePe or Paytm) with the payee's UPI ID and amount filled in. The payment happens entirely inside that UPI app; Ghar Hisab does not process, see or store any payment or bank details.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in Settings.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and app interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Permissions

  • Notifications — only for the reminders you turn on.
  • Internet — only used by the ads SDK.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a service inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all records from your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
