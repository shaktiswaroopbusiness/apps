Privacy Policy – GigaLeft

Last updated: 27 November 2026

GigaLeft ("the app") shows how much of your mobile data pack is left, which apps use your data, and your usage history. This policy explains what data the app reads and what happens to it.

In short: your usage data never leaves your phone. No account, no server, no tracking, no market research, no reading of your screen, messages or browsing.

What the app reads, and why

  • Data usage counters (Android's network statistics) — how many bytes the phone and each app used over mobile data, Wi-Fi and hotspot. Used to show data left, alerts, the per-app list and history.
  • App usage events (with the "Usage access" permission you turn on) — how many times an app was opened and for how long, shown on that app's detail page.
  • SIM information (with the phone permission, if you allow it) — the network name of each SIM (for example Airtel or Vi), its slot and which SIM is used for data. The app does not read your phone number, calls, call log or SMS.
  • Installed launcher apps — the list of apps with a home-screen icon, so you can pick apps for the app blocker.

All of this is processed only on your device. The app has no server and never uploads, sells or shares your usage data, app list or SIM information with anyone, including us.

What is stored

Your plan settings, alert choices and a daily usage history (kept for the number of months you choose, 1–12) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Android backup is turned off for this app, so this data is not copied to any cloud backup. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Live meter and notifications

If you turn on the live meter, the app runs a small foreground service that refreshes your data left in the notification bar and home-screen widget about once a minute. Alerts (for example at 80% used) are created on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.

App blocker (local VPN)

The optional app blocker uses Android's VPN feature only to stop the apps you choose from using the internet. It runs entirely on your phone: traffic from blocked apps is dropped on the device, other apps are not routed through it, and the app does not inspect, log, collect or send any network traffic. No data passes through any remote server.

Exporting your history

If you tap Export CSV, the app creates a file with your daily totals and opens the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes; the app does not upload it by itself.

Checking your balance

The Check balance button opens your phone's dialer with your carrier's balance code (for example *121#). You press call yourself; the app does not see the result.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and app interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Your data usage, app list and SIM information are never shared with the ads SDK. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

Permissions

  • Usage access — to read data usage and app opens (you turn it on in Android settings).
  • Phone (optional) — to show each SIM's network name.
  • Notifications — for the live meter and the alerts you turn on.
  • Foreground service, run at startup — to keep the live meter working after a restart.
  • VPN (optional) — only when you turn on the app blocker.
  • Internet — only used by the ads SDK.

The app does not request access to your contacts, SMS, call log, photos, camera, microphone or location, and it does not use accessibility services.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app to remove everything it stored from your phone. You can also turn off usage access or the phone permission at any time in Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
